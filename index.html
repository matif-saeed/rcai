About the committee

About RCAI

The Research Committee for AI & Data Science.

RCAI (Research Committee · AI & DS) maintains this hub as a central, information-sharing resource for students and faculty. It brings together upcoming national and international conferences, journals and calls for papers across artificial intelligence and data science.

What this site is: a curated directory of research venues, updated regularly, to help researchers decide where and by when to submit their work.

What this site is not: a submission portal. RCAI does not receive, review or process papers. All submissions are made directly on each venue's official website.

How the data stays current

All venue information lives in a single, plain-text data file (data.js). Committee members can add or update a venue in a few minutes — no web-development knowledge required. See the project README for step-by-step instructions.

Have a venue to add or a correction to suggest? Contact the committee to have it included in the next update.
